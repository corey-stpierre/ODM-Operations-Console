Important behavior

AD actions may take effect immediately. DoNotStartBefore is an earliest start, not a held task. Shared-profile changes affect every associated device. A successful submission does not certify a completed migration or every setting's readback. Review Quest jobs, runs and actual profiles before retrying.

Coverage limits

Quest remains required for administrative setup, environment/workflow design, agent deployment, new profiles and credentials, unsupported job controls, self-service templates, wizard-only settings and full vendor dashboards. Five M365 constructors remain explicit web handoffs. Hybrid SharePoint remains out of scope. This candidate does not claim exhaustive native web-console parity or defect-free behavior.

AD offline tests validate console logic against installed command metadata and real SDK models with a mocked service boundary. No compiled AD mutation cmdlet or live tenant operation was invoked.
